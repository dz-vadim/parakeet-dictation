## Applied now (behaviour-preserving; one commit each; all six suites green after each)

Ordered by payoff over risk.

1. VAD buffer as float32 hop chunks (like `_gap` already is): `_emit_segment` concatenates,
   `_SpeechSegment.samples` is an ndarray, `_remember_gap` takes the slice copy, `open_tail`
   concatenates chunks, `.tolist()` only at the sherpa `accept_waveform` boundary, capture
   loop passes the ndarray. Measured: 8x less RAM in a long phrase, ~5 ms/s less under
   `live_lock`, ~1–11 ms per segment close, ~3.5 ms per preview pass.
2. `app.main()` order: instance lock → controller → `preload()` first, then overlay / focus /
   hotkeys; `FocusTracker._load` asynchronous like `_recheck`. ~50–65 ms earlier model-ready
   and hotkey-live.
3. "Listening..." posted on the speech edge, not every 100 ms tick; streaming loop re-posts a
   partial only when it changed (prevents an unbounded typer queue in streaming per-segment).
4. Overlay frame timer only in animating states; `paused` ticks at 1 Hz; `error`/`success`
   draw once.
5. `FocusTracker._recheck` every 60 s instead of 10 s (idle wakeups of two processes).
6. Clipboard staging: the two `wl-paste` reads and the two `wl-copy` writes run concurrently
   (~7 ms off the release-to-text path).
7. `dbus.py` leaf (`session_bus()`, `call_sync(...)`) replacing five copies of the
   `Gio.DBusConnection.call_sync` body in instance/focus/hotkeys/insert;
   `PortalKeyboard.interface_present` goes through `_call`.

## Follow-ups (need a product decision, change config keys, or change what tests pin)

- **Remove the toggle / start-stop modes, the pynput listener and its four bindings, the
  Hotkeys capture grid, the pynput dependency (also from the PKGBUILD), per-segment
  insertion, `partial_overwrite` and the streaming partial-overwrite protocol** (~350 lines,
  7 config keys). pynput cannot grab keys on KDE Wayland; tray/SIGUSR1 toggling stays. With
  it, `enforce()` and the hold+per_segment invariant cease to exist instead of being guarded in
  four places. Decision pending from the user.
- Controller: replace `_stop_pending`/`_start_pending` with one `_key_down` written only by
  press/release (one untested edge changes: re-press during STARTING after a deferred release
  keeps recording); then a `_Take` object for the nine per-take fields; then a `_Session`
  object in the engine (stop event, thread, stats, abandoned) removing the `stop_event=None`
  dual source of truth.
- Engine-level queued session (`session_queued`/`session_skipped`, `previous` join) duplicates
  the controller's queued start; tests pin it.
- `enforce()` is at the wrong level: the forbidden thing is inserting while a key is held, not a
  config combination — move it to the take (`held=True` from `hold_press`).
- Clipboard restore on the paste worker (removes `_clip_gen`, the RLock, the pending flag);
  the Shift+Insert retry after a transport failure (a dead transport is tried twice).
- Remove the `wtype`/`xdotool` typer paths and the `typer` key; remove `_migrate_legacy_models`
  and `APP_DIR`; `TextTyper(config)` instead of eight kwargs (14 test call sites).
- `recognizer_key` becomes unnecessary once the dialog stops aliasing the config; tests pin it.
